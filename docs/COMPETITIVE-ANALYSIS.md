# Competitive Analysis — International School Management Software

> Research date: September 2026
> Purpose: Inform Eskoofy branding site design, pricing, and marketing strategy

---

## Executive Summary

Analysis of 8 leading school management software companies reveals clear patterns in homepage structure, pricing models, feature showcase approaches, and conversion strategies. The top 5 patterns Eskoofy should adopt:

1. **Public, transparent pricing** with tier comparison (openSIS/Fedena model)
2. **Dual conversion paths:** Free trial + Demo booking (openSIS/Fedena model)
3. **Role-based feature organization** alongside module-based (Alma model)
4. **Named testimonials with school logos + stats counters** (universal pattern)
5. **Niche positioning** for international schools with explicit school-size segmentation (Alma/Gibbon model)

## Recommendations for Eskoofy Branding Site

### Homepage
- **Hero:** Benefit-driven headline + clear CTA + product screenshot
- **Stats bar:** "X Schools, Y Countries, Z Students" for credibility
- **Feature preview:** Tabbed/accordion for quick exploration
- **Testimonials:** Named individuals with titles and school logos

### Feature Showcase
- Dedicated features page with mega-dropdown navigation
- Organize by module (SIS, Attendance, Fees) AND by user role (Admin, Teacher, Parent, Student)
- Include screenshots/mockups for each feature

### Pricing
- Public pricing page with clear tiers
- Monthly/Annual toggle with savings badge
- Consider staff-based pricing as differentiator
- Free tier or free trial to reduce friction

### Social Proof
- Named testimonials with titles and school names
- Client logo carousel + stats counters
- Software review badges (Capterra, G2, GetApp)
- Consider video testimonials for highest impact

### Conversion
- Persistent "Book a Demo" / "Start Free Trial" CTA in header
- Phone number in header
- Live chat / WhatsApp support
- "Switch from [Competitor]" migration messaging

### Content Marketing
- Blog with educational content
- Case studies with detailed stories
- Interactive product tours (Navattic/Storylane)
- Webinars for lead generation
