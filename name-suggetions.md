## Before You Decide

- Check domain availability (.com / .com.bd) and social handles
- Search trademark databases (DPDT Bangladesh, WIPO) and app stores
- Say the name out loud on a phone call — if it needs spelling twice, skip it
- Test it in both EN + BN UI: `site_ui('app.name')` will render it in the
  sidebar topbar and PWA manifest (`name` / `short_name`)
